C.1 Paleoclimatic data

C.1.1 Neogene paleotemperature layers used in climate reconstructions.

Spatially explicit reconstructions of mean annual surface temperature across the Northern Hemisphere from 23 to 2 Ma, shown at 1-Myr intervals and 1° × 1° spatial resolution. Each panel represents a single time slice, displayed using a common diverging color scale to facilitate comparison across time. These layers form the basis for estimating directional temperature change and climatic instability used in subsequent analyses.

C.2.0.11 Evaluating sampling effort

Sampling effort was evaluated as the relationship between the number of sampled fossil localities and observed generic richness for every stage in the Neogene. Sampling–richness relationships were examined within each Neogene stage to assess how efficiently additional localities contributed to the recovery of new genera and to identify stages showing evidence of saturation versus continued sampling dependence.
